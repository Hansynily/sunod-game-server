What the clear rate means

Clear rate = quests completed ÷ quests played, across every finished run. A quest is completed when the player solves it before its 30-second timer runs out; it fails when the timer runs out or they quit in the middle of it. Faster solves keep more stars (0-3).

How the clustering works

The model was trained on 10,000 people's answers to the 48-item Holland (RIASEC) questionnaire. K-Means grouped those people into 8 clusters of people who answered alike, and a Random Forest learned to tell which cluster a new set of 48 answers belongs to. A second Random Forest inside each cluster then picks the closest career (sub-group) in it.

In SUNOD, the 48 quests are the 48 questions: each quest's 1-5 score fills its item, and the cluster is predicted from the player's quests. Clusters 1 and 4 are "outlier" groups (people whose answers didn't fit a clear pattern) and have no careers, so a player landing there is redirected to the named cluster that best matches their scores.

How to read this profile

RIASEC is Holland's model of six interest types. Every quest belongs to one type, and every skill the player uses belongs to one type too. For each type the game adds up stars earned × 2 + quests completed × 3 + its share of skill use × 5, then scales the scores so the player's strongest type = 10.

So the numbers are relative to the player: a 10 is their strongest interest, a 5 is about half as strong. The three highest letters make their Holland code (e.g. SIA). The bars compare the types to each other, not to other players.
